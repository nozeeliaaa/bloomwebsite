Features

26+ tools to help you track, learn, predict, and find support.

Bloom combines practical, educational, and safety-focused features across six categories - built with Jamaican users at the centre of every decision.

📅 A - Track

📅

Cycle Calendar

View your full cycle at a glance - period dates, phases, predictions, and key events all in one visual calendar.

📝

Health Logging

Record flow intensity, cramps, moods, discharge, and other cycle-related symptoms to build a rich personal picture.

📓

Notes & Journaling

Add free-form notes to any day - useful for capturing details you might want to share with a healthcare provider.

📊

Dashboard Insights

Your personalised home view - showing your current cycle day, phase, and upcoming key dates at a glance.

🔮 B - Predict

🗓

Next Period Prediction

Bloom estimates your next period window based on your logged history and average cycle length.

🌙

Cycle Phase Insights

See estimated menstrual, follicular, ovulation, and luteal phases presented in a clear, approachable way.

🌿

Ovulation Estimate

Bloom estimates your likely ovulation window based on average cycle data and logged patterns.

🌸

Fertile Window

Bloom highlights your estimated fertile window - useful for both family planning and general awareness.

⚠

Missed Period Detection

Bloom notices when a period appears late based on your pattern and gently flags it so you can seek guidance.

📈

Cycle Trend Analysis

Over time, Bloom tracks cycle variability and patterns to give you a more accurate picture of your health history.

📚 C - Learn

💬

Bloomie Assistant

Get educational answers on reproductive health topics - always in a supportive, non-diagnostic role that encourages professional care.

📚

Health Library

Explore educational pamphlets covering cramps, PMS, PCOS, endometriosis, hygiene, pregnancy, fertility, contraception, and more.

🎮

Facts & Myths Game

An interactive educational quiz that challenges common misconceptions about reproductive health in a warm, stigma-free format.

❓

Help & Resources

In-app help guides and educational support to help users navigate Bloom and understand what its features offer.

📍 D - Find Support

📍

Clinic Finder

Search and browse Jamaican reproductive health clinics filtered by location and care type.

🏥

Clinic Details

View full clinic information including services offered, address, contact details, and opening hours.

🗺

Route Support

Get directions support to help users reach their chosen clinic from within the Bloom app.

🚪

Support Requests

Submit help requests directly within Bloom. Admins review and respond to ensure users get the support they need.

⚙ E - Manage

👤

Profile & Preferences

Set your health goals, preferred cycle tracking mode, and personal preferences from your profile settings.

📄

Exportable Summaries

Generate and export health summaries from your cycle data - useful for sharing with healthcare providers.

🔔

Notifications & Reminders

Receive timely reminders for logging, upcoming cycle events, and helpful wellness prompts.

🔒

Privacy Controls

Sensitive reproductive health data is handled carefully. Users maintain control over their information and how it is used.

🛡 F - Admin Tools

🖥

Admin Dashboard

A role-restricted overview for administrators to monitor platform activity, users, and support needs.

👥

Manage Users

Admins can view and manage user accounts, including account status and access levels.

📋

Manage Educational Content

Admins can add, edit, and remove educational pamphlets and health resources within the Health Library.

🏥

Manage Clinics

Admins maintain the clinic directory - adding new clinics, updating details, and removing outdated entries.

📩

Manage Support Requests

Admins review user-submitted support requests and respond within the app to ensure timely help.

🎯

Phase Feedback

Users can flag if a predicted phase feels inaccurate - helping Bloom improve estimates over time.

Why Bloom is different

More than a period tracker.

Bloom combines tracking, prediction, education, guided support, clinic access, and an interactive game into one culturally relevant platform - built specifically for Jamaica.

1

Jamaican context

Local clinic discovery and community-aware messaging throughout the app.

2

Safety boundaries

3

Bloom adapts its dashboard and insights to each user’s personal health goal.

4

Education built in

From the Health Library to the Facts & Myths Game, learning is woven into every part of Bloom.
