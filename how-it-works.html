How It Works

A simple journey from setup to full support.

Bloom is designed so you can get started quickly, understand your data clearly, and find the support you need - all at your own pace, in your own time.

User journey

Seven steps to a fuller picture of your health.

1

Create your account

Sign up securely through Firebase Authentication. Your account keeps your data private and accessible only to you.

2

Complete your health survey

Set your cycle details, health goal, and preferences. Bloom uses this to personalise your dashboard and insights from day one.

3

Log your cycle data

Add period start dates, flow, symptoms, moods, and notes. The more you log, the more accurate Bloom’s estimates become over time.

4

View your dashboard & calendar

See your current cycle day, estimated phase, next period prediction, ovulation estimate, and fertile window in a clear visual layout.

5

Learn through Bloom

Ask Bloomie questions, browse the Health Library, and test your knowledge with the Facts & Myths Game - all within the same app.

6

Find clinics & support

Use the Clinic Finder to locate Jamaican reproductive health services, get directions, and submit support requests if you need help.

7

Export & review your summaries

Generate a health summary from your cycle data - useful for bringing to medical appointments or reviewing your own patterns.

Behind the predictions

How Bloom generates cycle insights.

Bloom’s predictions are built on your own logged data - not guesswork. Here’s how the process works in simple terms.

1

Collects your period dates

Bloom uses the period start dates you log to understand your personal cycle history.

2

Calculates your averages

Average cycle length and average period duration are calculated from your logged data over time.

3

Estimates your current phase

Based on where you are in your cycle, Bloom estimates whether you are in the menstrual, follicular, ovulation, or luteal phase.

4

Predicts your next period

Using your average cycle length, Bloom estimates when your next period window is likely to begin.

5

Estimates ovulation & fertile window

Bloom estimates when ovulation is likely and flags the surrounding days as your potential fertile window.

6

Detects late or missed periods

When a period appears late based on your history, Bloom flags it and encourages you to seek guidance if needed.

7

Applies fallback logic

When data is limited, Bloom falls back to standard cycle averages and clearly shows when an estimate is based on less information.

8

Improves with more data

The more consistently you log, the more accurate Bloom’s estimates become. Even occasional logs help build the picture.

Medical disclaimer: Bloom provides educational cycle estimates only and does not diagnose medical conditions. Predictions are based on logged data and standard cycle averages - they are helpful guides, not guarantees. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for health concerns.
Admin journey

Role-restricted management for a safer platform.

Bloom includes a fully separate admin experience that allows authorised administrators to manage platform content, users, and support - keeping Bloom safe, current, and well-maintained.

1

Sign in as admin

Admins access a separate, role-restricted entry point secured by Firebase Authentication.

2

Admin dashboard overview

A summary view showing platform activity, pending requests, and key management areas.

3

Manage users

View registered user accounts, update access levels, and manage account status as needed.

4

Manage educational resources

Add, edit, or remove health pamphlets and resources in the Health Library to keep content current.

5

Manage clinic listings

Add new clinics, update existing details, and remove outdated entries from the Clinic Finder directory.

6

Respond to support requests

Review user-submitted support requests and respond within the app to ensure users receive timely help.

See Bloom in action

Designed to inform, empower, and connect.

Bloom brings together cycle tracking, educational resources, guided support from Bloomie, and local clinic discovery - all in one warm, approachable experience built for Jamaica.

  • The problem: stigma, confusion, and limited access to reproductive health information.
  • Bloom’s main tools: cycle tracking, Bloomie, the Health Library, and the Clinic Finder.
  • The value: safer learning, better awareness, and local support for Jamaican users.
