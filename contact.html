About the Project

A student-built reproductive health platform for Jamaica.

Bloom was designed and built to demonstrate thoughtful UI/UX design, frontend development, and socially impactful technology - created with Jamaican communities in mind as a university academic project.

🎓

Academic project

Built to showcase thoughtful design, full-stack development, and community-focused technology at a university level.

🌸

Our purpose

To help users understand their reproductive health, track their cycle, and find support - safely, privately, and without stigma.

⚕

Medical disclaimer

Bloom is for educational use only and does not replace advice from qualified medical professionals. Always consult a doctor for health concerns.

🚪 Need support as a Bloom user?

Support for the Bloom app is handled directly within the platform. Users can submit a support request from inside the app and an admin will respond in-app. This contact page is for project-level enquiries only.

🚨 Medical emergency? If you or someone else is experiencing a medical emergency, please contact emergency services or go to your nearest hospital immediately. Bloom is an educational platform and cannot provide urgent medical assistance.
